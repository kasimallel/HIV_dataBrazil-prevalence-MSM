# HIV Prevalence and Associated Factors Among Adolescent Men Who Have Sex With Men Aged 15–19 Years in Two Brazilian Cities: A Respondent-Driven Sampling Study

Replication dataset for the article above.

## Summary

Adolescent men who have sex with men (AMSM) remain vulnerable to HIV despite global declines in incidence. This cross-sectional biobehavioural survey used respondent-driven sampling (RDS) to estimate HIV prevalence and associated factors among AMSM aged 15–19 years in São Paulo and Fortaleza, Brazil.

| Item | Description |

| Population | AMSM aged 15–19 years |
| Setting | São Paulo and Fortaleza, Brazil |
| Design | Cross-sectional biobehavioural survey |

| Data collection | December 2020 to July 2022 |
| Sample size | 275 participants |
| Primary outcome | HIV infection (HIV testing) |
| Covariates | Sociodemographics, sexual behaviour, HIV risk perception, PrEP perceptions, mental health |
| Analysis | RDS-weighted prevalence; multivariable Poisson regression (adjusted prevalence ratios) |

RDS variables (participant ID, recruiter ID, coupon, network size, seed) are included to reproduce the weighted estimates.

## Main results

- RDS-weighted HIV prevalence: 2.7% (95% CI 1.1–6.1) overall; 6.3% (2.3–15.9) in Fortaleza; 2.3% (0.8–6.4) in São Paulo.
- HIV was associated with:
  - age 18–19 years (aPR 13.2, 1.5–115.3)
  - moderate or severe depressive symptoms (aPR 13.9, 1.6–124.8)
  - unprotected anal intercourse (aPR 10.6, 1.1–104.5)
  - discomfort that PrEP uses the same drug as HIV treatment (aPR 7.7, 1.8–32.6)
